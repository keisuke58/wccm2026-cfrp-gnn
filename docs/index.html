1 · Melt → cool → residual stress

Nakamura × CHILE × viscoelastic

On cooling from the melt, crystallinity α develops through a temperature window (Nakamura); it stiffens the ply and — with thermal + crystallisation shrinkage — builds residual σ₁₁, partly bled off by temperature/crystallinity-shifted relaxation.

2 · Why viscoelasticity matters

elastic vs. relaxed

A purely elastic cure calculation over-predicts residual stress. Thermal relaxation bleeds it off during the hot part of the cycle — the difference between a textbook estimate and a measurement-grade one.
